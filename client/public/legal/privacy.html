3. Privy

\n

Login is handled by Privy (Privy Technologies, Inc.). When you log in with Google, Apple, or email, Privy authenticates you and creates a NEAR wallet on your behalf. Privy's own privacy policy governs what Privy collects during authentication. We receive from Privy only the account identifier and the NEAR account ID it creates.

\n

**[LAWYER]** A data-processing agreement with Privy is required under Article 28 GDPR. Confirm whether one is in place or needs to be put in place.

4. NEAR testnet

\n

Game actions (the Drawing result, trades, item ownership, fines) are recorded on NEAR testnet, a public distributed ledger operated by NEAR Protocol. Data written to a public blockchain cannot be deleted. Before any game actions are written to NEAR testnet, players should be told this.

\n

**[LAWYER]** The interaction between GDPR deletion rights (Article 17) and immutable blockchain records is unsettled in EU law. Please advise on:

\n

1. Whether writing a player's NEAR account ID and transaction history to a public ledger constitutes processing of personal data.

\n

2. How to reconcile the right to erasure with the immutability of blockchain records (for example, whether off-chain pseudonymisation and on-chain hashed identifiers would be sufficient).

\n

3. Whether a DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment) is required before NEAR mainnet is used.

5. EU consent age and children

\n

ELEMENTS requires players to be 13 or over. In the EU, the age at which a child may consent to personal data processing (specifically: accessing information-society services) is set by each member state:

\n

| Member state | Age of digital consent |

\n

| --- | --- |

\n

| Germany | 16 |

\n

| Ireland | 16 |

\n

| Netherlands | 16 |

\n

| France | 15 |

\n

| Italy | 14 |

\n

| Most other EU states | 14 or 16 |

\n

| UK | 13 |

\n

A player aged 13 to 15 in a member state with a higher consent age cannot validly consent to their own data processing without a parent or guardian's authorisation.

\n

**[LAWYER]** Advise whether {company} must:

\n

1. Detect the player's member state at sign-up and apply the correct consent age.

\n

2. Obtain and verify parental consent for players below that state's threshold.

\n

3. If parental consent is required, what mechanism is sufficient (a checkbox ticked by a parent, email verification of a parent address, or an alternative verification step).
