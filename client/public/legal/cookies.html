Last updated: 2026-10-29

1. What storage ELEMENTS uses

ELEMENTS uses the browser's local storage and session storage. It does not set cookies in the traditional sense, but the ePrivacy rules that govern cookies apply equally to any storage written to a user's device.

The table below lists every item we write to the browser's storage.

Item Type Purpose Strictly necessary
Privy authentication token Local storage Keeps you logged in between sessions without asking you to log in again Yes
Game session state (current zone, last position) Session storage Restores your position if the page reloads mid-session Yes
Sound preferences (volume, muted) Local storage Remembers your audio settings Yes. Without it, the setting resets on every page load
Consent decision Local storage Records whether you have seen and responded to the consent banner Yes

We do not write any tracking, analytics, advertising, or session-replay data to your device.

2. Third-party storage

Privy may write its own items to local storage as part of the authentication flow. These are governed by Privy's own cookie and privacy policy.

We do not load any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. If this changes, we will update this policy and the consent banner before loading them.

3. How to manage storage

You can clear all browser storage at any time through your browser's settings. Clearing storage will log you out and reset your local preferences. Your account data on the game server is not affected.

4. Changes

If we add non-essential storage (for example, optional analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your consent before writing anything new to your device.
